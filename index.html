Who We Are

About Our Company

"Grab and Go" Order, relax and enjoy is a university food delivery service created to make ordering food from the university canteen easier, faster, and more convenient for students.

Our platform allows customers to view the menu, place orders, track their orders, and share feedback or complaints through one simple system.

We focus on quality food, accurate orders, timely delivery, customer satisfaction, and continuous improvement.

👁️

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and efficient campus food delivery service by providing quality food and an excellent customer experience.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide safe, quality and accurately prepared food to students on time through an efficient digital ordering and delivery system, while continuously improving our service based on customer feedback.

The Quality Management System covers the processes involved in receiving digital food orders from university students, coordinating food preparation, conducting quality checks, managing customer communication and feedback, and delivering food orders within the university campus premises.

Important Information

Conditions

⏰

Meal Times

Orders should be placed within the specified meal times: Breakfast 6:00–7:00 AM, Lunch 11:30 AM–12:30 PM, and Dinner 5:00–6:00 PM.

✏️

Order Changes

Customers can request changes to their order within 15 minutes of placing the order. After 15 minutes, changes may not be possible as the order may already be under preparation.

✅

Order Accuracy

Customers should carefully check their food items, quantities, and order details before confirming the order.
