Our Commitment

Quality Objectives & Policies

🎯 Quality Objectives

1

Timely Delivery

Our objective is to deliver at least 95% of customer orders within 20 minutes from the time the order is placed.

2

Customer Feedback & Complaints

Our objective is to provide an easy and accessible way for customers to share feedback and submit complaints through our dedicated Feedback & Complaints tab.

3

Order Accuracy & Food Freshness

Our objective is to achieve at least 98% order accuracy and ensure that 95% of delivered food meets the required freshness and quality standards.

📜 Quality Policy

1

Customer Satisfaction

We are committed to providing safe, quality and reliable food delivery services that meet customer requirements and applicable university and food-safety requirements.

2

Quality & Timely Service

We are committed to maintaining food quality, order accuracy and timely delivery through effective control of our ordering, food preparation, quality checking and delivery activities.

3

Continual Improvement

We are committed to continually improving our services and Quality Management System by monitoring performance, reviewing customer feedback and complaints, identifying problems and taking appropriate corrective actions.

How We Work

Process Methods

Core Key Processes

01

Customer Order Management & Web Platform Operations

Responsibility: Operations Manager & Web Platform Administrator
📥 Inputs
  • Customer order details via Website / Mobile Web App
  • Real-time Canteen menu availability data
  • Payment confirmation / authorization
📤 Outputs
  • Verified order ticket transmitted to Campus Canteen
  • Order confirmation notification sent to customer
  • Digital order log record
Process Steps
  1. Customer accesses website and selects menu items.
  2. Automated system verifies item availability with canteen inventory state.
  3. Customer submits payment and receives order confirmation number.
  4. Order detail is instantly routed to Canteen Kitchen Display/Terminal.
02

Food Preparation & Quality Assurance (Canteen Partner Joint Venture)

Responsibility: Canteen Kitchen Supervisor & Quality Assurance (QA) Inspector
📥 Inputs
  • Verified order ticket
  • Fresh raw food ingredients and recipe specifications
  • Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for food hygiene & temperature
📤 Outputs
  • Hygienically packed meal ready for dispatch
  • Quality verification checklist record
Process Steps
  1. Kitchen staff receives digital order ticket and prepares food per standard recipe/hygiene rules.
  2. QA/Kitchen Inspector verifies portion size, packaging integrity, and temperature.
  3. Meal is sealed with a tamper-evident seal and labeled with Order ID.
  4. Order status is updated to "Ready for Dispatch" on the system.
03

Order Dispatch, Delivery & Service Release

Responsibility: Delivery Fleet Supervisor & Delivery Personnel
📥 Inputs
  • Sealed meal package with Order ID label
  • Campus delivery location details & customer contact
  • Delivery thermal bags and transport equipment
📤 Outputs
  • On-time delivered meal to customer
  • Digital Proof of Delivery (POD) & Customer Rating/Feedback
Process Steps
  1. Delivery rider scans package QR/Bar code to accept delivery assignment.
  2. Package is placed inside temperature-controlled thermal carrier.
  3. Rider navigates to designated campus delivery point.
  4. Handover meal to customer, obtain digital confirmation, and log delivery completion.

Support Support Processes

01

Purchasing & Vendor/Canteen Relationship Management

Responsibility: Procurement & Supply Chain Officer
📥 Inputs
  • Packaging material requirements & food vendor evaluation criteria
  • Approved Supplier List & Canteen Joint Venture agreement terms
📤 Outputs
  • Purchase orders for packaging and eco-friendly delivery materials
  • Supplier/Canteen evaluation performance records
Process Steps
  1. Identify raw material / packaging needs and send POs to vetted suppliers.
  2. Receive goods and inspect compliance against standards.
  3. Periodically evaluate Canteen joint-venture quality compliance and vendor performance.
02

Warehouse, Stores & Inventory Management

Responsibility: Stores & Inventory Controller
📥 Inputs
  • Inbound delivery packaging materials, seals, rider gear
  • Storage temperature & hygiene guidelines
📤 Outputs
  • Properly stored packaging materials
  • Inventory stock movement log
Process Steps
  1. Inspect incoming non-perishable delivery items (bags, containers, uniforms).
  2. Store items in designated hygienic storage facilities following FIFO (First-In, First-Out).
  3. Issue packaging supplies to Canteen dispatch point based on order volumes.
03

Human Resource (HR) Management & Competency Development

Responsibility: HR & Training Manager
📥 Inputs
  • Staff job descriptions & required skill profiles (Riders, QA staff, Customer Support)
  • Training modules on Food Safety, QMS Quality Policy, and Customer Care
📤 Outputs
  • Competent, trained workforce
  • Staff training records & competency evaluations
Process Steps
  1. Recruit qualified riders, system administrators, and support staff.
  2. Conduct QMS orientation (Quality Policy, hygiene standards, customer focus).
  3. Evaluate post-training performance and maintain competency records.
